# Settld: 2-minute demo script

**Live app:** https://tabsplit-live.lovable.app · Big screen: `https://tabsplit-live.lovable.app/t/<CODE>/screen`
**Tagline:** *Splitwise leaves an IOU. Settld gets you paid, before anyone stands up.*

Everything below was tested end to end on the live site at 19:25 (host + guest on two devices).

## Before you go on stage
- Host phone: on `/new` → "Tonight's receipt" → pick the receipt photo once, so it's saved. Payment details are already remembered.
- Laptop on the projector, ready to open the table's **Big screen** link.
- Backup: open the table link with `?demo=1` → the host gets **🎭 Simulate table** (4 fake guests claim live). Say so if you use it.

## The script
| Time | Say / do |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | **Hook:** "Hands up if a group dinner ended with one person paying and then chasing everyone on WhatsApp for a week." |
| 0:15 | **AI reads the receipt:** host taps **Tonight's receipt** → items appear, checked against the printed total → **Open the table**. Put the **Big screen** up: giant QR + 4-letter code. |
| 0:30 | **Judges join:** "Scan it. No app, no sign-up." Their silly names (Noodle Ninja, Taco Tornado…) pop up on the big screen. |
| 0:40 | **Say what you had:** one judge types *"the lamb and half the daal"* → the AI ticks Lamb Biryani + a half share of the daal. Everyone else taps. Point at the red **"Who had the Okra Fries? 👀"** banner. |
| 1:00 | **Get paid:** host taps **🔒 Lock** → every phone shows **"You owe Faizan £X"** with Revolut + bank details, amount ready to copy. A teammate taps **I've paid** → ✅ on the host's Track screen. |
| 1:15 | **Table awards 🏆:** the AI roasts the table (*"Did you accidentally order the entire menu twice?"*). Read one out. |
| 1:25 | **Spin for it 🎡:** "Some tables don't split, they spin." Host → ⚙️ More → **Spin for it** (confirm) → **Spin**. Every phone in the room spins at once and lands on the same person: **"🎉 Tom pays £74.93"**. |
| 1:45 | **Close:** "Splitwise launched receipt splitting two weeks ago, so the problem's real. But it ends with an IOU. Settld ends with the host paid. And the host's home screen shows everything they're still owed, with one-tap WhatsApp chasing." |

## Likely questions (short answers)
- **How do you know they paid?** Honour system plus the host as referee tonight. Open Banking confirmation is the next step, and the business model.
- **How do you make money?** Guests paying the restaurant directly by bank from this screen, via a licensed provider: cheaper than card fees, and we take a cut of the saving.
- **Why no accounts?** That's the point: nobody installs or signs up, so a whole table joins in 5 seconds.
- **Isn't the wheel gambling?** It's the card roulette friends already play, made fair and visible. Anyone can sit out, and no money comes to us.
